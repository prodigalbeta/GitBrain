# Game Document
## Intro
- A fantascience CRPG that harkens back to the classic era of Ultima with 2D isometric graphics and tactical, turn-based combat on an isometric hex grid
## Features
- TTRPG Style Character Creation
- Classes & Battle Roles
	- Melee
	- Range
	- Healer
	- Tank
- Battle Party of 4
	- Wards or retainers??
- Biome based battle maps
	- Sets of maps for each biome that are randomly selected at the start of each battle
- MMO style boss fights
- Experience after a fight awarded into a pool for the party, the player then divides the experience amongst the party members as they want
- Loot and gear similar to diablo/borderlands
	- Ultra-rare drops need to be appraised before using
## Setting
- The decaying, dead body of Gaia floating in rim space
	- Uppity God-Emperor is trying to revive Gaia and turn her into a living warship to bring rim space under his rule
- 4 rim space systems not including Gaia
