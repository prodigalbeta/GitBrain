# Intro
- A fantascience CRPG that harkens back to the classic era of Ultima with 2D isometric graphics and tactical, turn-based combat on an isometric hex grid
## Features
- TTRPG Style Character Creation
- Classes & Battle Roles
- Party of 4
	- Wards or retainers??
- Biome based battle maps
	- Sets of maps for each biome that are randomly selected at the start of each battle
- MMO style boss fights
## Setting
- The decaying, dead body of Gaia floating in rim space
	- Uppity God-Emperor that needs to be taken out
	- Gaia is invaded??
